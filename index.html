Robotics Research Engineer · MEXT Scholar

Hi, I'm Wasuthorn Ausrivong (Won).

Wasuthorn Ausrivong is a Master's student at Osaka University under Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship, specializing in climbing robots, industrial inspection robotics, autonomous locomotion control, and bio-inspired robotics. Formerly a Senior Robotics Research Engineer at VISTEC, Thailand, he developed robotic systems for pipeline inspection, furnace cleaning, and magnetic wall climbing. His research has been presented at leading international robotics conferences and published in peer-reviewed journals, contributing to the advancement of autonomous robotic technologies for industrial inspection and maintenance.
